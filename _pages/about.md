        <span>01</span>
        <h3>Primordial Black Holes</h3>
        <p class="lang-en">Connecting gravitational-wave observations with primordial black-hole abundance, merger histories, and dark-matter scenarios.</p>

  <section class="academic-section academic-section--profile" id="profile">
    <div class="profile-text">
      <p class="section-kicker">
        <span class="lang-en">Academic Profile</span>
        <span class="lang-zh">学术简介</span>
      </p>
      <p class="lang-en">Zu-Cheng Chen is an associate professor in the College of Physics and Electronic Science at Hunan Normal University. His work sits at the interface of gravitational-wave astronomy, theoretical cosmology, and compact-object physics, with a focus on primordial black holes and low-frequency gravitational-wave probes of the early universe.</p>
      <p class="lang-zh">陈祖成现任湖南师范大学物理与电子科学学院副教授。其研究处于引力波天文学、理论宇宙学与致密天体物理交叉领域，重点关注原初黑洞以及低频引力波对早期宇宙的探测。</p>
      <p class="lang-en">He is a member of LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA, PPTA, and IPTA scientific collaborations, and serves as a referee for journals including PRL, PRD, SCPMA, and MNRAS.</p>
      <p class="lang-zh">他是 LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA、PPTA 和 IPTA 等国际科学合作组成员，并担任 PRL、PRD、SCPMA、MNRAS 等期刊审稿人。</p>
